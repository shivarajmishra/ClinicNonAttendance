Breast screening attendance risk dashboard: a patient list with computed non-attendance risk scores, filterable by risk tier, with per-patient factor breakdowns and recommended interventions.

Screening non-attendance risk dashboard

Flags patients at high risk of missing their breast screening appointment, using relative risk ratios published in Ellis et al., Lancet Public Health 2017.

How to use this dashboard
  1. Add patients. Use "Add patient" for one at a time, or "Import from Excel/CSV" to bring in a whole clinic list at once.
  2. Review the list. "Upcoming appointments" is sorted by risk score by default — click a column heading to re-sort, or use the tier chips (Low / Moderate / High / Very high) to filter.
  3. Open a patient. Click a row to see their risk gauge, factor breakdown and suggested actions in the "Patient detail" panel.
  4. Act on high-risk patients. Each tier lists suggested interventions drawn from the study's findings — treat these as a starting point, not a fixed protocol.
  5. Edit or remove a patient at any time from the row actions on the right of the table.
  6. Check the "Clinic summary" tab for an aggregate view of the currently loaded list — risk tier mix, deprivation and rurality breakdowns.

Importing from Excel or CSV

Upload a spreadsheet with one row per patient (.xlsx, .xls or .csv). Column headers don't need to match exactly — the importer looks for common names such as "Age", "SIMD", "Delay (days)", "LGA", "Practice deprivation", "Distance (km)" and "Prior DNAs". LGA values can be typed as the local government area name (e.g. "Liverpool"). You'll see a preview of the detected columns and which rows could and couldn't be parsed before anything is added to the list. Download a CSV template.

How the risk score works

Each patient's risk index multiplies the relative risk ratios (RRRs) from Ellis et al. for their age band, deprivation decile, appointment delay, local government area, mean practice deprivation and distance to practice. See the footnote below the dashboard for the full formula and important caveats about calibration, including how LGA weighting was adapted for SWSLHD.

Data storage

Patient records stay in this browser only — nothing is uploaded to a server. "Clear all" removes them permanently, so use it deliberately.

Reflects only the patients currently loaded in this browser, not a full practice register. Deprivation decile 1 = most deprived, 10 = least deprived. LGA risk weighting is an unvalidated approximation — see the note below the main dashboard.

Risk index = exp(sum of ln(RRR) across age, deprivation decile, appointment delay, local government area, mean practice deprivation and distance to practice), each relative to the study's reference category. Weights are taken directly from the study's adjusted negative binomial model and are not yet calibrated to local screening attendance data — treat tiers as a provisional starting point and recalibrate once local outcomes accrue. The local government area factor originally used Scotland's 8-level urban/rural classification; it has been remapped to SWSLHD's 6 LGAs (Canterbury-Bankstown, Liverpool, Fairfield, Campbelltown, Camden, Wollondilly), ordered most to least urban and reusing 6 of the study's published RRRs in that order. This is an unvalidated approximation — under Australia's own remoteness classification, almost all of SWSLHD sits within "Major City," so treat this factor's contribution to the score with particular caution until it is locally recalibrated.
